# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 6, 2026

## 1. Data Collection

We collect the following data when you use ShopZone:

### Order Information
- Customer name
- Delivery address
- Contact details
- Order history
- Product preferences

### Technical Data
- IP address
- Browser type
- Pages visited
- Access time/date
- Device information

## 2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:
- ✅ Process and deliver orders
- ✅ Communicate with you via WhatsApp
- ✅ Improve our service
- ✅ Prevent fraud

**We DO NOT:**
- ❌ Sell your data to third parties
- ❌ Share personal information without consent
- ❌ Use data for marketing without permission

## 3. Data Protection

Your data is protected by:
- ✅ HTTPS encryption in transit
- ✅ Secure storage practices
- ✅ Limited access controls
- ✅ Regular security audits

## 4. WhatsApp Integration

When you use WhatsApp integration:
- Your message is sent directly to our WhatsApp number
- WhatsApp processes this data per their privacy policy
- We receive order notifications on our WhatsApp Business account

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:
- ✅ Access your data
- ✅ Correct inaccurate data
- ✅ Request deletion
- ✅ Data portability
- ✅ Withdraw consent

**Contact:** support@shopzone.com (or your contact email)

## 6. Data Retention

- **Orders:** Kept for 3 years for legal/tax purposes
- **Account Data:** Retained while account is active
- **Deleted Data:** Permanently removed after 30 days

## 7. Third-Party Services

We use:
- **WhatsApp** - For order confirmations (WhatsApp Privacy Policy applies)
- **GitHub Pages** - For hosting (GitHub Privacy Policy applies)

## 8. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be notified via email or banner notification.
